# Sourcing Alert: GlobalTech Components Bankruptcy

**Supplier:** GlobalTech Components (Southeast Asia)
**Component Dependency:** Tier 1 Microchips
**Status:** Chapter 11 Proceeding Issued

We received notices that our primary microchip supplier in Southeast Asia, GlobalTech Components, has formally filed for bankruptcy protection following catastrophic capital management errors. This freezes their current outbound shipping queue.

**Action Required:**
We rely on GlobalTech for 65% of our Tier 1 telematics microprocessors. Alternative sourcing protocols must be enacted immediately. Preliminary graphs cross-referenced with historic supplier capability indicates that 'NeoCircuits Inc' (based in Taiwan) has the manufacturing line availability to undertake our volume, albeit at a 12% premium. Our procurement agents must immediately draft RFPs to transition sourcing pathways before our buffer stock of 45 days is depleted.
