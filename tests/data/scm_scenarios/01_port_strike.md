# URGENT ALERT: Port of Antwerp Dockworker Strike

**Date:** 2026-11-15
**Region:** EMEA Logistics Hub
**Impact Level:** SEVERE

A massive dockworker strike has commenced at the Port of Antwerp, entirely freezing our primary European logistics gateway. Initial estimations suggest that our raw material bulk carriers arriving from South America will be stalled for a minimum of 14 days in anchorage. 

**Lead Time Degradation:**
The standard container offload window has expanded from 48 hours to a projected 21 days. Consequently, our European manufacturing dependencies are now facing an immediate disruption to Justin-Time (JIT) delivery pipelines. We must immediately investigate rerouting inbound maritime cargo to the Port of Rotterdam, though Rotterdam is concurrently experiencing 85% capacity utilization.
