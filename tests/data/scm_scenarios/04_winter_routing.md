# Transport Disruption: Rocky Mountain Winter Storms

**Date:** February 2
**Scope:** North American Intermodal Network

Severe, historic winter storms across the Rocky Mountains have caused critical disruptions to our rail transport network. Over 400 miles of track operated by BNSF and Union Pacific are buried under three feet of snow, causing catastrophic avalanches on secondary transit routes.

**Logistical Constraints:**
Over 250 of our priority containers carrying time-sensitive perishable composites are stationary. Because the multi-modal transport routing relied on rapid rail-to-truck transition points in Colorado, our secondary truck freight teams are sitting completely idle, collecting demurrage. A rapid re-optimization of multi-modal routes to shift the cargo south through Texas is necessary, utilizing expedited cross-country trucking teams to bypass the rail blockages entirely.
