# Inventory Audit: EOQ Miscalculation (Part #AC-994)

**Auditor:** Logistics Financial Team
**Period:** Q3

A critical error was discovered in the Economic Order Quantity (EOQ) formula used by our automated procurement system for the Alternator Coil (Part #AC-994). For the past six months, the system was configured with an annual Holding Cost percentage of 5% instead of the accurate regional warehouse rate of 22%. 

**Consequences:**
Due to this miscalculation, our facilities have been over-ordering the Alternator Coil, purchasing in large batch quantities under the faulty assumption that storage was inexpensive. This has led to an extreme supply glut, occupying 15% of total high-value warehouse space safely reserved for volatile electronics. An immediate algorithm correction is required, alongside a liquidation schedule for the overstock.
